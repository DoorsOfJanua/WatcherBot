## Design Context

### Users
WatcherBotRoom is Janua's private desktop workspace for working with a small team of autonomous bots. He uses it frequently and wants to understand results, review a few consequential actions, and move on without managing the machinery underneath.

### Brand Personality
Compact, warm, and decisive. The bots should feel like capable teammates in a beautiful personal room, not developer tools, dashboards, or terminals.

### Aesthetic Direction
Refined desktop messaging with quiet, tactile surfaces and strong typographic hierarchy. Support the existing Midnight, Atelier, Foundry, and Lagoon skins, while avoiding code-heavy blocks, nested panels, generic card grids, excessive rounded boxes, and persistent technical metadata.

### Design Principles

- One ask, one decision: batch related work into a single review moment.
- Results first: show the human meaning; keep commands, tool names, and diagnostics behind optional detail.
- Scan in five seconds: concise headings, short rows, and one unmistakable primary action.
- Earn every surface: plain conversation stays plain; a container appears only when it enables selection, editing, approval, or recovery.
- Trust through receipts, not interruptions: routine safe work proceeds under standing permission and reports what happened afterward.
